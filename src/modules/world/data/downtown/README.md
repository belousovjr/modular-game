# Chicago Loop: reproducible city data

Bounded central district: west −87.642°, south 41.877°, east −87.6195°, north 41.8915°.
Approx. 1.86 × 1.61 km: Loop, river junction and nearest north-bank landmarks. This is a first central district, not the whole municipal downtown boundary. Origin −87.63075°, 41.88425°; east +X, north +Z; metres. Extension changes the input snapshot/bbox, not the renderer.

## Map source

© OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/), [attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
`osm-snapshot.xml.gz` is the original OSM-derived deduplicated snapshot dated 2026-11-07 from the neighbouring project's source cache, used with the owner's permission. Contributor metadata/unrelated POIs were removed there. It contains source nodes/ways/relations; no character model or animation was copied. The snapshot SHA-256 is recorded in `city.json.source.osmSha256`. The snapshot, generated map database and `public/maps/downtown.svg` are OSM-derived and distributed under ODbL. Application code is separate. The source needed to regenerate this database is included.

Road polylines keep actual curves, one-way tags, connected nodes, bridges and separate mapped carriageways. Width uses OSM width where available, otherwise lanes × 3.2 m plus 3 m (bounded 6…36 m); this is an estimate. Sidewalks are generated by polygon buffers/unions/differences, not a survey of every curb. Underground/tunnel/negative-layer ways are omitted; all levels of Lower Wacker are not reproduced. Above-ground layer metadata is retained, but a complete multilevel junction graph is not implemented. Road overlaps are unioned before clipping to chunks. Crosswalks/signals at connected junctions are procedural estimates, not verified positions of every signal in Chicago. The signals are visual props, with no traffic simulation yet.

Building anchors, footprints and available height/levels are taken from OSM. Heights without tags are explicitly estimated. Ordinary buildings are extruded from their clipped OSM polygons, including concavities and holes, and batched by tile/material. Landmark reusable envelopes are shrunk to fit. Overlapping footprints are excluded. This avoids roads/river/other buildings, but is not an exact reconstruction of each facade or every tagged building part. Landmark profiles Willis/Trump/Marina/Wrigley/Tribune are procedural approximations, guided by the neighbouring project and the photo sources below. No human assets were taken from that project.

## Relief and river

`elevation.json` caches 81 official [USGS 3DEP EPQS](https://epqs.nationalmap.gov/v1/json) samples, fetched 2026-11-16 with raster ID/resolution per sample. Local vertical datum subtracts 180 m; a 9×9 bilinear grid feeds all land, roads, foundations and collision triangles. The samples range 175.59…192.87 m. Dry land is clamped to +1.5 m to keep coarse water/shore samples from submerging roads; this is a deliberate approximation, not centimetre-accurate terrain or a replacement for a dense DEM.

River outline comes from OSM polygons/relations. Land is cut out, bank walls close the vertical gap, bridge edges extend below the deck. Water is −3.5 m in local coordinates, an artistic reference parameter, not a current gauge reading. Bed depth follows the neighbouring reference's 1:1 near-bank slope, smoothly approaching 18 m, with small sediment relief; it is **procedural bathymetry, not measured soundings**. Bed is triangulated on an 8 m grid and remains geometry below the water; sediment shading reuses the existing concrete detail texture with a brown tint, without allocating another texture. Water uses small animated normals without reflection/refraction scene renders. There is no swimming mechanic.

## Offline preparation

Python 3.12 + dependencies pinned in scripts/downtown-requirements.txt (Shapely 2.1.2 including its NumPy dependency), only for preparation; none is a browser dependency:

```
python3 scripts/fetch-downtown-elevation.py  # only when the cached elevation source is absent
python3 scripts/prepare-downtown.py
```

district.json holds the bounded district configuration. For expansion, edit its bounds/origin, run fetch-downtown-osm.py and refresh the cached elevation, then regenerate. The preparation command is offline and deterministic. Polygon unions, differences, constrained triangulation, collision envelopes, lane/crosswalk markings, signal/lamp placement and 128 m chunks are prepared once, outside the render loop. Existing axis-aligned River North generation is retained for compatibility/tests; the active `world` now consumes the polygon city source. Generated counts and height provenance remain inspectable in city.json. Every triangulation checks area conservation. Actual shader/LOD/streaming performance is measured separately on the GPU.

## Street/facade refinement

Street widths estimated from incomplete lane tags are normalised along each named street/direction/layer (explicit OSM widths are preserved). Rounded union outlines and a common 32 m triangular height grid avoid curb/sidewalk twists and mismatched interpolation. The sidewalk top and curb top share the same height; the curb includes an actual 18 cm vertical face. Coordinate noise is simplified with 6 cm tolerance. These widths remain estimates, not a curb survey.

Ordinary buildings use fitted wall UVs: each edge holds a whole number of window columns and the full wall height holds whole rows. Plinth, roof/cornice and two floor-aligned belts on the longest facade are plain architectural surfaces, encoded in the same UV buffer/material batch. No mesh or texture is allocated per window or belt. Indexed quads and roof caps reduce repeated vertices. Footprint simplification stays inside the original polygon with 12 cm tolerance. Remaining fine architectural details are a separate artistic task.

## Coarse map grid (explicit user preference)

`district.json.grid` defines 1 m source/object anchors, 0.25 m dimensions and 15° module headings. OSM projection now rounds the map nodes too; this intentionally prioritises a regular Chicago adaptation over exact survey geometry. Buildings are transformed as whole polygons and fitted into nearby free cells, retaining source IDs and collision agreement. Generated intersections/triangulation vertices and interpolated ramps remain continuous so that the snap does not introduce cracks. Quarter-metre terrain samples, 0.25 m curb rise/width and 3.25 m sidewalks share the same height sampler. Decal depth bias is a technical exception, not an authored dimension.

All final non-emissive world materials now use the shared CC0 albedo/AO and matching normal textures, including roof/cornice/base roles. Per-role roof/concrete/metal samplers are shared; no map is generated from brightness or allocated per building. See public/textures/city/README.md for the baked AO process and provenance. Curbs retain their own brighter tint after texture loading, rather than becoming identical to sidewalks.

## Traffic layout refinement

Connected nearby junction nodes are clustered before crossing placement; a two-arm way split does not create an intersection. Each 3 m deep crossing is accepted as a complete band with sidewalk endpoints, 0.5 m longitudinal bars and gaps, and no overlap with another band. Stop lines respect incoming one-way approaches. Unsupported tight corners omit a whole crossing instead of scattering partial bars. Layout metadata is retained for regression checks; this is a visual adaptation, not a surveyed or certified traffic design.

Signal supports use the sidewalk 0.75–1.75 m from the asphalt; adjustable mast arms place heads over the road facing approaching traffic, preferring the far side. The arm remains in the distant LOD. Geometry is still baked by tile/material and props reuse existing rendering batches. References: [FHWA MUTCD 11th edition](https://mutcd.fhwa.dot.gov/pdfs/11th_Edition/mutcd11thedition.pdf), [Babylon scene optimisation](https://doc.babylonjs.com/features/featuresDeepDive/scene/optimize_your_scene/).

Support seating: pole roots use the continuous sidewalk height with 0.125 m burial and the minimum terrain height under their foot, rather than rounding Y upward to the authoring grid. Lamp feet must fit entirely on the sidewalk. Facade belts now follow the recessed wall edge, end short of corners, overlap the wall and project only 0.0625 m; their complete footprint is checked offline against the building polygon. No extra meshes, materials or triangles are introduced by this repair.

River exclusions: interior holes up to 64 m² are filled before deriving dry terrain, bank walls and bathymetry. These small map exclusions do not supply pier heights; extruding them to DEM land height previously produced false tall pillars beside bridges. Larger islands remain. Dedicated bridge/protection piers require explicit verified geometry rather than generic terrain extrusion.

## Review district: deliveries 13–14

`review-route.json` pins Clark Street, Randolph and Daley Plaza, plus roof/skyline views; its dry walking polyline is checked in headless tests. The world review scenario uses these views through the existing temporary-camera runner. Ordinary buildings keep source-derived volumes and heights. Their roofs now sit behind a 0.25 m parapet; suitable roofs receive one small service enclosure. Street-facing closed entrance portals use a 2.5×2.75 m opening, frame and handles; the plinth is split below them and feet follow the actual terrain rather than an assumed sidewalk rise. These are procedural architectural cues, not surveyed doors, accessible interiors or exact recreations of each facade.

Additional portal/roof geometry shares existing facade/foundation/metal/roof materials, matching normal maps and baked AO. It is batched by tile/material, streamed at existing street distances and casts real shadows. Distant primary building envelopes remain intact. Lighting and facade filtering are documented in module instructions; no per-window material/mesh or streetlight render targets were added. Hardware review conditions, including an inconsistent first measurement, are recorded in docs/PERFORMANCE_BASELINE.md.

Door portals, frames and handles have been removed at the user’s request. Plinth faces are continuous again; roof plant, parapets and first-floor glazing remain. This supersedes the earlier entrance description and review captures.

## Aggressive geometry and texture LOD

Ordinary buildings now keep simple inset coarse prisms in the 128 m skyline batches (19230 triangles). Full facades, trim and rooftop enclosures live in optional per-building `chunks[].details`, loaded at 24 m/unloaded after 32 m, with a 3D visibility cutoff at 32 m. The opaque coarse core stays behind the near shell; no overlapping exposed planes are intentionally used. Collision footprints remain full-resolution and independent of rendering. Landmark templates shed decor at 24 m and use strongly reduced distinctive silhouettes from 80 m. No new materials/textures are allocated. SurfaceScale adds up to +3 mip bias between 12 and 80 m for both albedo/AO and normals; these mips remain resident, so this is not texture streaming or a VRAM-freeing claim.

Height-aware disappearance is configured by `district.json.buildingLod` and requires regeneration after edits: buildings below 45 m use 180/220 m load/unload, 45–100 m use 400/480 m, and taller ordinary buildings retain 1200/1400 m. Coarse mesh batches are split by both material and height category, enabling actual scope/geometry disposal on horizontal departure. A 3D visibility check also hides low/mid groups when flying high, retaining the horizontally loaded buffers until the streaming scope unloads. Distances are measured to group bounds with hysteresis; collisions remain persistent. Landmark range stays unchanged.

Window alignment across LOD: coarse facade UVs project onto reference edges of the detailed facade and share its floor origin/storey scale, rather than rounding a new grid for the coarse body. Near window U begins at an integer offset (128) to reserve negative U for material roles; the offset does not change periodic window phase. Coarse UVs can be fractional, and negative V padding is unwindowed. The main shell separation is now 0.0625 m to reduce parallax. Non-fitted landmark windows use stable SurfaceScale metre coordinates (2.8×3.5 m cadence) instead of each box's changing dimensions.
